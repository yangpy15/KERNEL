# Clinical Feasibility Evaluation Questionnaire

## Introduction

Thank you for participating in this evaluation study.

You will sequentially review clinical cases from the emergency departments of **Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center in Boston, USA**, and **Stanford Health Care in the USA**. The evaluation includes a total of **10 clinical cases** from **2 datasets**.

In this questionnaire, you will assess whether the laboratory test recommendations and corresponding explanations generated by the AI model for each case are accurate, reasonable, and clinically useful.

This study is conducted solely for academic research purposes. All case data have been processed in accordance with applicable research requirements. Your responses will be collected anonymously and used solely for research analysis and academic publication. No personally identifiable information will be collected, and the evaluation results will not be used for actual clinical diagnosis or treatment.

This study has been approved by the Taipei Medical University Joint Institutional Review Board (TMU-JIRB Protocol No.: N202510021). Participation is voluntary. By indicating your consent below, you agree to participate in this study and allow your anonymous responses to be used for the research purposes described above. If you do not consent, you will not be able to proceed to the questionnaire.
